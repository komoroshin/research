The eight-day test scale as pill dots — the product's whole notion of progress during a test.

```jsx
<DayDots pattern="dddstaaa" />
<BodyText size="xs" strong>day 5 of 8 · without dairy</BodyText>
```

d = done (sage), t = today (forest), a = ahead (2px ring), s = skipped/not counted (cream fill + ring). A skipped day must never read as a failure.

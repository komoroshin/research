Oswald quantity display — use for every number the product reports (12 / 15, 60%, 125 ml, 4 h 40 min) with an `Eyebrow` caption underneath.

```jsx
<Numeral size="xl" unit="/ 15" dark>12</Numeral>
<Eyebrow tight tone="dark">rough days with dairy</Eyebrow>
```

Sizes: xl 48, lg 40, md 34, sm 30, xs 22, 2xs 16. `unit` renders at half size in sage. Rule of the brand: numbers large, explanations small.

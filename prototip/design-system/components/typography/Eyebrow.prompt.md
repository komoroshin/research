Uppercase, wide-tracked micro-label — Threshold's only label style; use it for section labels, screen top rows, and captions under numerals.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Background for the day</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="dark">Observing</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tight tone="forest">rough days with dairy</Eyebrow>
```

Tones: `default` (ink-600), `soft` (sage-600, quietest), `dark` (sage-500, on forest), `forest` (accent, paired with a numeral). `tight` drops tracking from 0.18em to 0.12em when the label captions a number. Never use it as a headline — headlines are `Display`.

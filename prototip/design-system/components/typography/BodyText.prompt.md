Inter running text at six steps — everything that isn't a headline, numeral, or label.

```jsx
<BodyText>Dairy showed up in 12 of your 15 rough days.</BodyText>
<BodyText tone="soft" size="2xs">Pick one in each row. You can change it later.</BodyText>
```

Tones: default (ink-700), soft (ink-600), dark (sage-100 on forest), muted (sage-500), paper (near-black, doctor page only). Caveat copy ("this could be a coincidence") must stay at `md` — never demote it more than one step.

Uppercase tag for a food group on a recognised meal — informational, never interactive, never coloured by verdict.

```jsx
<Chip>lactose</Chip>
<Chip tone="sage">fructans</Chip>
```

Pick the tone that contrasts with the card it sits on: `cream` on sage/accent cards, `sage` on cream/quiet cards. A meal with no known group shows a single `—` chip at 60% opacity.
